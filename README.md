**Plot**
In Middle-earth, the Hobbit Frodo Baggins is tasked with destroying the One Ring—the source of the Dark Lord Sauron's power by throwing it into the fires of Mount Doom. While Frodo and Sam journey to Mordor guided by Gollum, their allies (Aragorn, Legolas, Gimli, and Gandalf) rally the free kingdoms of Men to fight Sauron's armies, buying Frodo enough time to complete the quest and save Middle-earth.

**Settings**
Middle-earth during the Third Age: a high-fantasy world featuring peaceful countryside (The Shire), ancient Elven havens (Rivendell), majestic kingdoms of Men (Rohan, Gondor), and the volcanic wasteland of Mordor.
